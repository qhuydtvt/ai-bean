# Visual Specification: Shhh... Woof!

This document defines the style, colors, and character designs to maintain visual consistency across all illustrations for the pilot project *Shhh... Woof!*.

## Base Illustration reference
- Spread 1 Illustration: [toby_and_quiet_cat.png](toby_and_quiet_cat.png)

## 1. Style & Art Direction Constraints
- **Art Style**: Painterly digital painting mimicking textured wet oil/acrylic brushes.
- **Texture**: Visible canvas textures and expressive brushwork; backgrounds have a softly smudged, painterly look to create a shallow depth of field.
- **Edges**: Form-thinking over line-thinking. Avoid black lineart outlines; sculpt forms using color blocks and light.
- **Lighting**: Cinematic volumetric lighting. Warm golden highlights from a defined source (sunlight, window, lamp).
- **Shadows**: Deep cool blue-violet shadows. Never use pure black or grey shadows.
- **Margins & Bleed**: Maintain a `0.125"` to `0.25"` safe zone. Keep critical faces and text elements clear of the outer 10% of the canvas.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Dominant Tones**: Soft warm browns, warm cream/wood tones, and sunny golden highlights.
- **Accent Tones**: 
  - Starry night blue (Toby's pajamas)
  - Bright yellow-gold (stars, sunshine, bird)
  - Pure white (cat's fur)
  - Rich brown & white (dog's coat)
  - Deep blue-violet (shading & shadows)

## 3. Character Specifications

### Toby (Protagonist)
- **Age**: 3 years old.
- **Hair**: Fluffy, messy medium-brown hair with soft, painted highlights.
- **Face**: Rosy cheeks, tiny nose, big dark eyes, and a sweet, curious expression.
- **Outfit**: Two-piece long-sleeve pajamas in deep starry-night blue, printed with yellow five-pointed stars.
- **Consistency Anchor**: Always wears the blue pajama set with yellow stars.

### Quiet Cat
- **Fur**: Extremely fluffy, clean white fur with visible soft textures.
- **Eyes**: Big green eyes.
- **Unique Mark**: A single, solid black patch covering its **left ear**.
- **Consistency Anchor**: Left-ear black patch.

### Noisy Dog
- **Look**: Playful, happy brown puppy with floppy dark-brown ears, a white patch on its chest/belly, and a wagging tail.
- **Accessory**: A simple, bright red collar.
- **Consistency Anchor**: Red collar.

### Quiet Mouse
- **Look**: Tiny gray field mouse with large, round pinkish ears and small shiny black eyes.
- **Prop**: A single sunflower seed held in its tiny paws.
- **Consistency Anchor**: Always holding a small seed.

### Noisy Bird
- **Look**: A small canary-like bird with soft, sunny-yellow feathers.
- **Unique Mark**: Bright sky-blue feathers on its wingtips.
- **Consistency Anchor**: Blue wingtips.
