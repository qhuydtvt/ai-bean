# Storyboard: Shhh... Woof!

**Format**: Short-form
**Target Age**: Ages 2–5
**Total Word Count**: 153 words
**Beat Count**: 5 beats across 5 spreads

---

## Front Matter

**Page 1** (Title Page)
- *Text*: *Shhh... Woof!* by Toby's Team
- *Art direction*: Toby is seen standing in the middle of a split illustration: on the left, he is whispering "Shhh" next to a sleeping cat; on the right, he is laughing and jumping under a barking dog. Bright, pastel colors.

---

## Spread 1 — Meeting the Cat (Beginning)

**Pages 2–3**

**Story Text**:
> Toby walks very softly. *Tiptoe, tiptoe.* 
> Under the kitchen chair, Toby sees a soft, sleeping ball of white fur.
> A quiet cat stretches its pink paws.
> "Meow," whispers the quiet cat. *Meow, meow, meow.*
> Toby holds up one finger. "Shhh... quiet cat."

**Art Direction**:
- Composition: On the left page, Toby (viewed from behind) is creeping on tiptoes towards a wooden kitchen chair. On the right page, a close-up under the chair shows the fluffy white cat stretching out its paws, looking sleepily at Toby.
- Perspective: Medium shot on the left page, shifting to a low-angle close-up on the right page.
- Movement direction: Left-to-right (Toby moves towards the right side of the spread).
- Color/mood: Warm, soft kitchen lighting. Morning sunbeams filtering through a window.
- "So What?": The illustration shows the contrast of Toby's oversized shadow tiptoeing versus the small, delicate paws of the cat, emphasizing the scale and quietness.

**Layout**:
- Visual pacing: Quiet (simple kitchen floor background, focus on the chair and cat).
- Gutter safety: The kitchen chair legs are safely placed on the left, and the cat is centered on the right page. The gutter crease splits the floor empty space.
- Foreshadowing: Through the window in the background of the left page, a red fence is visible outside, hinting at the next setting.
- Page-turn hook: Toby is looking out towards the back door on the right page, prompting the reader to follow him outside.

**Character Sheet**:
- **Toby**: A curious 3-year-old boy in blue pajamas with yellow stars. Messy brown hair, rosy cheeks, and large, curious eyes. Consistency anchor: blue star pajamas.
- **Quiet Cat**: Fluffy white cat with one black patch on its left ear and green eyes. Consistency anchor: black patch on left ear.

---

## Spread 2 — The Noisy Dog (Middle)

**Pages 4–5**

**Story Text**:
> Toby runs out to the green yard. *Pitter-patter, zoom!*
> Near the red fence, a floppy tail wags back and forth, back and forth.
> A noisy dog chases a yellow butterfly.
> "Woof!" barks the noisy dog. *Woof, woof, woof!*
> Toby hops up and down. "Oh, noisy dog!"

**Art Direction**:
- Composition: Toby is bursting out of the open back door on the left page, arms wide in mid-run. On the right page, a playful brown puppy is jumping high in the air, trying to catch a glowing yellow butterfly near a red wooden fence.
- Perspective: Wide-angle panoramic shot covering both pages.
- Movement direction: Left-to-right (Toby running towards the dog; the dog jumping up and right).
- Color/mood: Bright, energetic daytime colors—lime green grass, bright red fence, sunny blue sky.
- "So What?": The dog's ears are flying wild and its tail is a blur of motion lines, visually showing the high noise and excitement level that matches Toby's jumping.

**Layout**:
- Visual pacing: Busy (lots of motion lines, butterflies, grass blades, and flying dirt).
- Gutter safety: The open back door is on the far left. The dog and fence are on the far right. The gutter crease splits the open grassy meadow safely.
- Foreshadowing: In the top corner of the right page, a branch of an oak tree dips down, hinting at the bird scene.
- Page-turn hook: The yellow butterfly is fluttering off the right edge of the page, leading the eye to turn the page.

**Character Sheet**:
- **Noisy Dog**: Happy brown puppy with long floppy ears, a white belly patch, and a bright red collar. Consistency anchor: red collar.

---

## Spread 3 — The Quiet Mouse (Middle)

**Pages 6–7**

**Story Text**:
> Toby walks back inside. *Sshh, sshh, quiet steps.*
> Behind the long blue curtain, a tiny gray nose wiggles.
> A quiet mouse nibbles a tiny seed.
> "Squeak," whispers the quiet mouse. *Squeak, squeak, squeak.*
> Toby curls up small. "Shhh... quiet mouse."

**Art Direction**:
- Composition: On the left page, Toby stands near the living room door, crouching and peering at a long blue curtain. On the right page, Toby pulls back the curtain slightly to reveal a tiny mouse sitting on its haunches, holding a sunflower seed.
- Perspective: Medium shot on the left, extreme close-up on the right showing the mouse and Toby's huge eye reflecting in the light.
- Movement direction: Left-to-right (Toby moves toward the curtain).
- Color/mood: Cool, quiet tones. Deep blue curtains, shadows, soft indoor lighting.
- "So What?": The illustration focuses on the mouse's delicate whiskers and the crumbly seed, highlighting tiny details that require quiet focus to notice.

**Layout**:
- Visual pacing: Quiet (large blocks of blue curtain and soft shadows).
- Gutter safety: The curtain crease aligns close to the gutter, but all critical mouse details are shifted to the center-right of the page.
- Foreshadowing: Through the living room window next to the curtain, branches of the oak tree are visible.
- Page-turn hook: Toby is looking upwards toward the top window pane, preparing the reader to look up.

**Character Sheet**:
- **Quiet Mouse**: Tiny field mouse with big round ears, dark shiny eyes, and a long tail. Consistency anchor: always holding a small seed.

---

## Spread 4 — The Noisy Bird (Middle)

**Pages 8–9**

**Story Text**:
> Toby looks out the high window. *Stretch, stretch, stretch.*
> Up in the oak tree, bright yellow wings flutter and shake.
> A noisy bird sings a happy song.
> "Tweet!" chirps the noisy bird. *Tweet, tweet, tweet!*
> Toby waves his hands. "Hello, noisy bird!"

**Art Direction**:
- Composition: On the left page, Toby is standing on a footstool, stretching his hands up to press his nose against the window glass. On the right page, we look from outside the window at the bright yellow bird perched on a branch, notes of music floating in the air.
- Perspective: Low-angle looking up from the garden up towards the window and the tree branch.
- Movement direction: Upward and left-to-right.
- Color/mood: Late afternoon sun, golden hour orange-yellow sky reflecting in the window pane.
- "So What?": Colorful, swirling music notes flow from the bird's beak, physically representing the song's noise and cheerfulness.

**Layout**:
- Visual pacing: Busy (tree leaves, branches, swirling music notes).
- Gutter safety: The window frame is on the left page. The bird on the branch is on the right page. The gutter splits the empty air between the house wall and the tree.
- Foreshadowing: The golden hour lighting indicates that sunset is coming, preparing the reader for bedtime.
- Page-turn hook: The sun is setting on the horizon on the far right page, signaling the end of the day.

**Character Sheet**:
- **Noisy Bird**: Small canary-like bird with fluffy yellow feathers, blue wingtips, and an orange beak. Consistency anchor: blue wingtips.

---

## Spread 5 — Bedtime (End)

**Pages 10–12**

**Story Text**:
> Now the sun goes down. *Yawn, yawn, yawn.*
> Toby snuggles under his warm, cozy blanket.
> "Meow," whispers the quiet cat on the rug.
> "Squeak," whispers the quiet mouse in the wall.
> "Woof!" barks the noisy dog in his basket.
> "Tweet!" chirps the noisy bird in her nest.
> Toby closes his eyes. *Sleep, sleep, sleep.*

**Art Direction**:
- Composition: A warm, cozy bedroom scene showing Toby asleep in bed. In four separate small, soft-focused circular vignettes around the bed, we see the four animals asleep in their respective beds (cat on the rug, mouse in a matchbox bed behind the baseboard, dog curled in a basket, bird tucked in its nest).
- Perspective: High-angle medium shot of the bedroom, offering a comforting view of the entire room.
- Movement direction: Static/peaceful (no active left-to-right movement).
- Color/mood: Deep indigo night sky outside the window, soft warm yellow nightlight glow inside the room.
- "So What?": The vignettes bring all characters back together, showing that quiet and noisy animals alike are now united in peaceful sleep, providing a comforting, secure resolution.

**Layout**:
- Visual pacing: Quiet (soft focus, dark borders, peaceful sleeping poses).
- Gutter safety: Toby's bed is centered on the left page. The vignettes are split on the right page. Gutter splits empty bedroom floor space.
- Foreshadowing: None (Final Spread).
- Page-turn hook: None.
